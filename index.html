06 · Hecho en Argentina

From the barrio court
to your screen.

Topper is an Argentine brand. In the 90s its high-tops were all over the country's courts, school gyms, and streets, worn by kids who grew up watching the Liga Nacional.

The Venom High II was originally designed by Paulo Ibarra.
